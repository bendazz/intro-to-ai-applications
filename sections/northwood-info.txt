Northwood Community Center — Quick Facts

HOURS
Monday to Friday: 6:00 am to 9:00 pm
Saturday: 8:00 am to 6:00 pm
Sunday: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm
The center is closed on national holidays.

MEMBERSHIP
Individual membership is forty dollars a month.
Family membership (up to five people) is sixty-five dollars a month.
Members under 18 must have a parent or guardian sign the membership form.
Memberships can be paused for up to two months per year at no charge.

CLASSES
Yoga: Tuesday and Thursday mornings at 7:00 am.
Water aerobics: Monday and Wednesday evenings at 6:30 pm.
Youth basketball: Saturday mornings at 9:00 am, for ages 8 to 14.
Classes are included with membership; no separate sign-up fee.

RULES
Members must scan their membership card at the front desk on entry.
Guests must be signed in by a member and may visit twice per month.
No pets are allowed inside, except for trained service animals.
Lost-and-found items are kept at the front desk for thirty days.

CONTACT
Front desk phone: 555-0147.
The front desk is staffed during all open hours.
