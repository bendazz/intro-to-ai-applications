Westview High Robotics Club — Member Handbook (Excerpt)

MEETINGS
The club meets every Tuesday and Thursday from 3:30 to 5:30 pm in Room 214.
Members are expected to attend regularly.

MEMBERSHIP
Membership is open to all students in grades 9 through 12.
Annual dues are $20, payable in the first month. A signed membership form,
including a parent or guardian signature, is required before a member may use
any equipment.

CODE OF CONDUCT
Treat teammates, mentors, and equipment with respect.
Power tools may only be used with a mentor present.
Clean up your workstation before you leave.

SAFETY
Safety glasses must be worn at all times in the build area.
Report any injury, however minor, to the club advisor immediately.

ELIGIBILITY
Members who miss four or more meetings in a semester may lose eligibility to
travel with the competition team, at the advisor's discretion.
